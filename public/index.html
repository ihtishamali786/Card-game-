How to upload to Google Play Console:

  1. Click Download upload_certificate.pem above to save the file to your computer or phone.
  2. Open your Google Play Console > Solitaire Hyper Card Games.
  3. Go to Release > Setup > App Integrity > App Signing tab.
  4. Under Request upload key reset, click the blue link Request upload key reset.
  5. Choose reason: "I lost my upload key" or "I want to use a new upload key".
  6. Upload the downloaded upload_certificate.pem file and submit.
